Privacy & Cancellation Policy

Samaritan Webtech Services • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Service Overview

Samaritan Webtech provides a local property management web platform designed for landlords to monitor active yards, track room occupancy statuses (active/inactive), and keep records of tenant rent payments.

2. Rental Payments & Deposits

All rent and deposit transactions processed through our integrated payment gateway are collected on behalf of the respective landlord or property manager.

  • Landlord Scope: The platform serves as a tracking system. Once funds are successfully transferred through the gateway, lease agreement cancellations and rental refunds must be negotiated directly with the landlord.
  • Duplicate Transactions: If a payment error or duplicate charge occurs during processing through the gateway, the user can submit proof of payment for verification and prompt refund of the excess charge.

3. Cancellation & Platform Fees

Subscriptions or service fees charged for access to Samaritan Webtech tracking tools are non-refundable once platform features are accessed, except in cases of verified technical system failures.

4. Data Privacy & Protection

We respect user privacy. Tenant payment tracking metrics, landlord room statuses, and login credentials (such as Samaritan IDs) are stored securely and used exclusively to provide property tracking services on the platform.
